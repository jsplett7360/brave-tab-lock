Set up Tab Lock

Tab Lock locks this browser profile behind a PIN after inactivity, when the browser starts, or when you press the shortcut. While it is locked, each tab shows a lock screen instead of its page; unlocking puts every tab back on its page, so you stay signed in and Brave Containers keep working.

1. Choose a PIN or passphrase

A PIN is easier to remember, so it’s the default. A passphrase is much stronger: there are only a million 6-digit PINs, so someone who copies this profile’s files from your computer can try them all offline. Whichever you choose, consider creating a recovery code in the next step.

2. Recovery code (recommended)

A recovery code resets your PIN if you forget it, and still opens the tabs Tab Lock is hiding. It is made on this device and never leaves it.

1. Enter your master PIN

This Mac’s installer set a master PIN for every Brave profile. Enter it once so this profile can encrypt your tabs when it locks.

Tab Lock is on

By default it locks after 5 minutes of inactivity, whenever the browser starts, and when you press Control+Shift+L or click the toolbar button. Change this in settings.

Recommended next steps

  • Private windows: open brave://extensions, choose Tab Lock → Details, and turn on “Allow in Private”.
  • Address bar suggestions: the address bar can still suggest sites from your history. To turn that off, open brave://settings/appearance and turn off “Show autocomplete suggestions in address bar”.
  • Other profiles: each browser profile has its own Tab Lock with its own PIN, unless the macOS installer set a master PIN.

What Tab Lock can’t do

  • It is a convenience layer. Your computer’s lock screen and disk encryption are the real protection.
  • Anyone who can read this profile’s files can try PINs offline; a 6-digit PIN falls quickly. Use a passphrase.
  • Tab titles and addresses may already be stored in the browser’s history and session files on disk. Hidden tabs also keep their pages in their Back history, and pressing Back on a locked tab can show the page for a split second.
  • Someone can open a different browser profile, make a new one, or use another browser.
  • If a page has to reload when you unlock, anything typed but not submitted on it is lost. (Lockdown mode, under Settings → Advanced, always closes tabs.)
  • Tab Lock can be removed from the browser’s extensions menu unless the macOS installer added it.
