Set up Tab Lock

Tab Lock locks this browser profile behind a PIN after inactivity, when Brave starts, or when you press the shortcut. While it is locked, your open tabs are encrypted, closed, and reopened when you unlock.

1. Choose a PIN or passphrase

A passphrase is much stronger. There are only a million 6-digit PINs, so someone who copies this profile’s files from your Mac can try them all offline.

1. Enter your master PIN

This Mac’s installer set a master PIN for every Brave profile. Enter it once so this profile can encrypt your tabs when it locks.

Tab Lock is on

By default it locks after 5 minutes of inactivity, whenever Brave starts, and when you press Control+Shift+L or click the toolbar button. Change this in settings.

2. Recommended next steps

  • Private windows: open brave://extensions, choose Tab Lock → Details, and turn on “Allow in Private”.
  • Address bar suggestions: the address bar can still suggest sites from your history. To turn that off, open brave://settings/appearance and turn off “Show autocomplete suggestions in address bar”.
  • Other profiles: each Brave profile has its own Tab Lock with its own PIN, unless the macOS installer set a master PIN.

What Tab Lock can’t do

  • It is a convenience layer. Your Mac’s lock screen and FileVault are the real protection.
  • Anyone who can read this profile’s files can try PINs offline; a 6-digit PIN falls quickly. Use a passphrase.
  • Tab titles and addresses may already be stored in Brave’s history and session files on disk.
  • Someone can open a different Brave profile, make a new one, or use another browser.
  • Locking closes your tabs, so anything typed but not submitted in a form is lost.
  • Without the macOS installer, Tab Lock can be removed from Brave’s toolbar menu.
